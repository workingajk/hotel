About Hotel Transylvania

Where Comfort Meets Timeless Hospitality

Our Story

Established in 2012, Hotel Transylvania has become one of the region's premier luxury destinations, offering exceptional hospitality to travelers from around the world. Blending elegant architecture with modern comforts, our hotel provides a peaceful retreat for families, couples, and business guests alike.

Every room is thoughtfully designed to provide comfort, convenience, and unforgettable experiences. Whether you're visiting for business, a family vacation, or a relaxing weekend getaway, our dedicated staff is committed to making your stay truly memorable.

Services

Luxury Spa & Wellness Fitness Center Restaurant & Dining Outdoor Swimming Pool Free High-Speed Wi-Fi Airport Pickup & Drop

Relax and rejuvenate with our full-service spa offering massages, aromatherapy, body treatments, and wellness therapies. Our experienced therapists ensure a peaceful and refreshing experience for every guest.
Stay active during your stay with our modern fitness center equipped with treadmills, exercise bikes, free weights, strength-training machines, and personal workout spaces.
Enjoy a variety of local, Indian, Asian, and international dishes prepared by our expert chefs. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and room service are available daily.
Take a refreshing swim in our temperature-controlled outdoor pool or relax on comfortable poolside loungers while enjoying drinks and snacks from the pool café.
Stay connected with complimentary high-speed wireless internet available throughout the hotel, including guest rooms, restaurants, conference halls, and outdoor areas.
We offer convenient airport transportation with comfortable vehicles and professional drivers to ensure a smooth arrival and departure experience.
